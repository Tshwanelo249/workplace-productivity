# Prompt Engineering Examples

## Email generation
Role: workplace writing assistant. Task: draft a professional email. Context: recipient, purpose and key details. Constraints: do not invent facts; use placeholders for missing details. Output: subject line and email body.

## Meeting summarisation
Role: meeting-notes assistant. Task: summarise notes. Output: summary, decisions, action table and unresolved questions. Constraints: do not invent owners, dates or decisions.

## Task planning
Role: project-planning assistant. Task: break a goal into manageable tasks. Output: task, priority, effort, dependencies and order. Constraints: state assumptions and risks.

## Research assistance
Role: research-planning assistant. Task: prepare a research plan and outline. Constraints: do not fabricate citations, links, statistics or quotations; flag claims requiring verification.

## Workplace chatbot
Role: focused workplace assistant. Task: answer a question using non-confidential context. Constraints: ask clarifying questions, do not invent policy, and flag uncertainty.

## Reflection
Clear roles, context, output formats and constraints make prompts more useful. Human review remains necessary.
